# **Jenson USA SQL Analysis**
## **Project Overview**
This project focuses on analyzing customer behaviour, staff performance, inventory management, and store operations at Jenson USA. By implementing SQL queries, we derive insights to optimize business strategies, improve staff performance, streamline inventory, and enhance customer experience.

## **Key Questions & SQL Queries**
### **Customer Insights:**
- Find the customer who spent the most money on orders.
- Identify customers who have ordered from all product categories.
- List products that have never been ordered using EXISTS.
### **Sales Performance:**
- Calculate the total number of products sold by each store along with store names.
- Find the product with the highest total sales (quantity * price) for each category.
- Find the top 3 most sold products by quantity.
- Calculate the cumulative sum of quantities sold for each product over time.
### **Staff Performance:**
- Identify staff members who have not made any sales.
- Find staff members who have made more sales than the average number of sales by all staff members.
### **Inventory Management:**
- Find the highest-priced product for each category.
- List the median value of the price list to understand price distribution.
### **Store Operations:**
- Find the total number of orders placed by each customer per store.
## **Key Insights**
- **Top Products & Sales Trends:** Identifying products with the highest sales and customers who spend the most helps in targeting marketing efforts, optimizing inventory, and boosting sales.
- **Underperforming Staff:** Identifying staff members with no sales can guide training and reward programs, fostering a motivated and efficient sales team.
- **Customer Behavior:** Understanding customers who purchase from all categories allows for the development of personalized marketing campaigns and loyalty programs.
- **Inventory Alignment:** Monitoring cumulative sales data and identifying top-selling products ensures that inventory is aligned with customer demand and prevents stockouts of popular items.
## **Recommendations**
- **Training Programs:** Implement training programs for underperforming staff and reward high performers to drive overall sales improvement.
- **Targeted Marketing:** Create personalized offers for top customers and promote top-selling products to increase revenue.
- **Inventory Management:** Discontinue products that have never been ordered to optimize inventory space and ensure that top products are always in stock.
